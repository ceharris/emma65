# Changelog

All notable changes to `emma65` (the emulator library, plus the `emma65` and
`emma65-tracer` binaries) are documented here, together with `emma6k-core`, the
CPU-neutral core crate (`core/`) that `emma65` builds on. The two are versioned
in lockstep and released together; `emma6k-core` has no changelog of its own.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this crate follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html)
— see `.claude/skills/release/reference.md` for this crate's specific
breaking/additive/fix-only definitions and pre-1.0 policy.
